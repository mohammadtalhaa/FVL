✍️ Writing Tips for Realistic Voices

  • ✅ Write conversationally — use contractions (don't, can't, won't)
  • ✅ Include natural pauses (three dots...)
  • ✅ Keep sentences short and natural
  • ❌ Avoid overly formal language
  • ❌ Don't use complex jargon

Example: "Hey everyone! So I've been testing this product, and honestly? It's pretty great. Here's what I really liked about it..."
