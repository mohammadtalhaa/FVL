What Are AI Voices?

AI voices are generated using advanced deep learning models trained on thousands of hours of studio audio. They improve over time and adapt to new languages and accents.

Advanced AI Technology

  • WaveNet Architecture — Generates raw audio waveform directly, producing highly natural speech patterns and context-aware intonation.
  • Attention Mechanisms — Focuses on important words and phrases, improving pronunciation accuracy and handling complex sentences.
  • Neural Vocoder — Converts acoustic features to smooth audio, removing robotic artifacts for natural transitions.

✍️ Best Practices

  • ✅ Use proper punctuation for natural pauses.
  • ✅ Break long sentences into shorter ones.
  • ✅ Use contractions for natural flow (don't, can't).
  • ❌ Avoid ALL CAPS (unless intentional emphasis).
  • ❌ Avoid ambiguous terminology without context.

Pro tip: For technical content, first use full names then acronyms (e.g., "HTML, which stands for HyperText Markup Language").
