FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is Free Voice Labs?
Free Voice Labs is an AI voice platform for generating studio-quality speech, blending voices, creating two-host podcast episodes, and cloning voices from a short sample.
How does AI voice generation work?
Your script is processed by neural text-to-speech models that convert text into natural-sounding audio, with control over delivery style, speed, and pitch.
What languages are supported?
Free Voice Labs supports 90+ languages across our neural and studio voice libraries.
What is voice cloning?
Upload a short voice sample (5–60 seconds) and our model clones its characteristics, so you can generate new speech in that voice.
Can I cancel my subscription?
Yes — both the weekly and monthly plans can be cancelled anytime from your account.
What audio formats are supported?
Generated audio can be downloaded as WAV for broadcast-quality use or MP3 for faster, smaller files.
