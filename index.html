Privacy Policy

Last updated July 2026

What we collect

We collect your email address, generated script text, and usage metadata (such as voice selected and timestamps) to operate the Studio and your history.

How we use it

Your data is used solely to render audio, maintain your account and history, and improve voice routing. We do not sell your data to third parties.

Audio storage

Generated audio and uploaded voice samples are stored securely and are accessible only to your account and, where required for support, our admin team.

Your rights

You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting support.
