Getting Started

What is Free Voice Labs?
Free Voice Labs (freevoicelabs.com) is a free text-to-speech converter that transforms written text into realistic, natural-sounding audio using AI-powered voice technology. Perfect for content creators, audio producers, and anyone who needs high-quality voice-over.
Do I need to sign up to use Free Voice Labs?
No! You can use Free Voice Labs without creating an account as a guest. Sign up to save your generation history and unlock additional features.
Is Free Voice Labs really free?
Yes! Our Free plan is permanently free with unlimited guest access. We also offer Pro ($4.99/month) and Lifetime ($29.99 one-time) plans for additional features and priority access.

Voice & Audio Features

How many voices does Free Voice Labs support?
We support 20+ professional voices including American, British, Japanese, Mandarin, Spanish, French, Hindi, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese, and more. New voices are added regularly.
Can I adjust the voice characteristics?
Yes! Use our Speed and Pitch sliders to customise each voice. We also offer 8 delivery style presets: Studio, Whisper, Narration, Promo, Story, News, Conversational, and Excited.
What audio formats are supported?
Generated audio is available as MP3 (for neural voices) and WAV (for Pro voices), compatible with all major platforms including TikTok, YouTube, podcasts, and more.
How long can my scripts be?
Guest users have no character limit! We handle long-form content efficiently. For very long scripts, the service automatically chunks and processes them.

Troubleshooting

Why is the audio quality poor?
Try using simpler, shorter sentences; check text for special characters; try a different voice or delivery style; or clear your browser cache.
The voice sounds robotic — what can I do?
Try the "Story" or "Narration" delivery styles for more natural-sounding audio. Adjusting the speed slightly (−5% to +5%) can also improve naturalness.
Why is my generation failing?
Possible causes: browser compatibility issues (try Chrome or Edge), very large text blocks (try breaking into smaller sections), special characters, or network issues. Try a modern browser and ensure a stable internet connection.
