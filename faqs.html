Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Free Voice Labs — from getting started to troubleshooting. Can't find what you're looking for? Contact support.

🚀 Getting Started
What is Free Voice Labs?
Free Voice Labs is a free AI text-to-speech platform that transforms written text into natural-sounding audio using neural voice technology. Perfect for content creators, podcasters, educators, and anyone who needs high-quality voiceovers.
Do I need to sign up to use Free Voice Labs?
No! You can use Free Voice Labs without creating an account as a guest. Sign up to save your generation history, access premium features, and manage your subscription.
Is Free Voice Labs really free?
Yes! Our Free plan is permanently free with unlimited guest access. We also offer Pro ($4.99/month) and Lifetime ($29.99 one-time) plans for additional features, priority generation, and voice cloning.
🎙️ Voice & Audio Features
How many voices does Free Voice Labs support?
We support 50+ voices including neural and studio voices across 90+ languages. The roster includes American, British, Japanese, Mandarin, Spanish, French, Hindi, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese, and many more. New voices are added regularly.
Can I adjust the voice characteristics?
Yes! Use our Speed and Pitch sliders to customise each voice. We also offer 8 delivery style presets: Studio, Whisper, Narration, Promo, Story, News, Conversational, and Excited.
What audio formats are supported?
Generated audio is available as MP3 (for neural voices) and WAV (for studio voices), compatible with all major platforms including TikTok, YouTube, podcasts, and more.
How long can my scripts be?
Guest users have no character limit! We handle long-form content efficiently. For very long scripts, the service automatically chunks and processes them for you.

🔧 Troubleshooting
Why is the audio quality poor?
Try using simpler, shorter sentences; check text for special characters; try a different voice or delivery style; or clear your browser cache.
The voice sounds robotic — what can I do?
Try the "Story" or "Narration" delivery styles for more natural-sounding audio. Adjusting the speed slightly (−5% to +5%) can also improve naturalness.
Why is my generation failing?
Possible causes: browser compatibility issues (try Chrome or Edge), very large text blocks (try breaking into smaller sections), special characters, or network issues. Try a modern browser and ensure a stable internet connection.
